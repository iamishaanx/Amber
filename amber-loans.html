Common questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The consulting call has no fees and no charges, and there is no obligation to take a loan.

What is the difference between secured and unsecured loans?

Secured loans, like home or car loans, are backed by an asset and usually have lower rates. Unsecured loans, like personal loans, need no collateral but may cost more.

How will I know if I qualify?

Submit the form and you'll know in up to 5 minutes. We send the result to your email and by text.

What documents might I need?

Usually identity proof, address proof, income proof and bank statements. Requirements differ by loan and lender, and your advisor will tell you exactly what's needed.
